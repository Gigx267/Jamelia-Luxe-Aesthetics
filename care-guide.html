Optimal Results & Safety

Treatment &
Aftercare Guide.

Proper pre-treatment preparation and post-treatment care ensure the smoothest recovery and long-lasting, elegant aesthetic results.

Step 1

Pre-Treatment Preparation

Follow these recommendations 24 to 48 hours prior to your scheduled appointment.

Avoid Blood Thinners

Refrain from taking aspirin, ibuprofen, or fish oil supplements 24–48 hours prior (unless medically prescribed) to reduce risk of bruising.

Stay Well Hydrated

Drink plenty of water before dermal filler or skin booster procedures to help hyaluronic acid absorb and bind moisture effectively.

Limit Alcohol Intake

Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours prior to treatment to minimize localized swelling and inflammation.

Arrive Clean Skinned

Please arrive with minimal or no heavy makeup on the targeted treatment areas to facilitate a smooth cleansing process.

Step 2

Post-Treatment Care

Key guidelines to protect your results and ensure smooth healing after your session.

Avoid Touching or Rubbing

Do not rub, press, or massage the treated area for at least 24 hours (especially after anti-wrinkle injections or fillers) to keep the product positioned precisely.

Avoid Extreme Heat

Skip saunas, hot tubs, steam rooms, intense sun exposure, and strenuous workout sessions for 48 hours following treatment to reduce localized swelling.

Use Sun Protection

Apply a high broad-spectrum SPF (30+) daily, especially after skin boosters or microneedling, to safeguard your skin during the healing phase.

Have Questions During Recovery?

Mild redness, small swelling, or light bruising can be normal for a few days post-procedure. If you experience severe discomfort or have questions, contact our clinical care team immediately.
